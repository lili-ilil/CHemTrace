How to read this page

82 items are listed. 54 are developer assumptions, set for this tool without a published source; 20 of them are marked high priority for expert review because they drive key results.

BasisMeaningItems
PublishedEquation or value from a peer-reviewed or official international source16
Swiss dataMeasured in Switzerland or taken from a Swiss agency report10
Legal valueSet in Swiss ordinances; check the current text2
Developer assumptionSet by the developer for this tool; needs expert review. Priority High Medium Low shows how much it moves key results54

Most values can be changed in Advanced mode, and entering measured data replaces the default. The equations are listed under "Screening equations and assumptions" on the platform, and the comparison with measurements is on the validation page.
