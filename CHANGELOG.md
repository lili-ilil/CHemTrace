# CHemTrace – changelog

Version history of the CHemTrace website (kept here, not on the website).

| Version | Date | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| 0.14.0 | 25 Nov 2026 | Five new metals: cadmium, copper, zinc, nickel and chromium, in Explore (all 13 compartments, pathways, condition effects) and Advanced mode. Kd for Cd, Cu, Zn and Ni from Sauvé et al. (2000) within the Allison & Allison ranges; chromium as Cr(VI) and Cr(III) with the Cr(VI) share set by soil drainage and aquifer redox, Cr(OH)3 solubility cap for Cr(III). Swiss values updated: TBDV amendment of 19 June 2026 (Pb 5 µg/L by 1 Feb 2036, total Cr 20 µg/L), AltlV As 10 µg/L and Pb 300 mg/kg in gardens; VBBo trigger values by land use (Cu fodder only); LRV Cd and Zn; EU target value for Ni. Health checks for Cd (TWI), Cu (ADI), Zn (US EPA RfD), Ni (TDI), Cr(III) and Cr(VI) (TDI). Soil atlas layers for Cd, Cu, Zn, Ni, Cr in grid.bin (22 layers); background of all eight metals at map sites. Forms by phase, PHREEQC salts and minerals for the new metals; glacier melt stays As, Hg, Pb only. Release pattern always shown; fixed to Continuous with the reason for groundwater, river, road and glacier releases, and single events only applied to soil and air (also in the HYDRUS export). Aerial image (SWISSIMAGE) switch on both maps. Manual: how each metal is calculated, two new examples; validation: plausibility checks for the new metals; 97 assumptions; 22 automated checks. |
| 0.13.0 | 3 Nov 2026 | Redox ladder now reports the predominant terminal electron-accepting process (O₂, nitrate, Mn(IV), Fe(III), sulfate, methanogenesis) and says it is not a species calculation. Organic contamination (fuel or oil, landfill leachate, sewage or manure, other organic carbon) enters only as an electron donor. New Water analysis tab: measured major ions, alkalinity, Fe²⁺, Mn²⁺, sulfide, temperature and Eh go into the PHREEQC file. PHREEQC export: arsenate added as a pH-matched sodium salt and NaOH as pH control for lead with measured alkalinity, so the batch converges and holds pH in all 48 test cases. Forms note: species distributions need thermodynamics with measured pH and concentrations. |
| 0.12.5 | 31 Oct 2026 | Logo without background: red CH stays, "emTrace" follows the theme (near-black in light mode, white in dark mode); favicon without background; logo files for light and dark backgrounds in assets/img. |
| 0.12.4 | 31 Oct 2026 | New CHemTrace logo (red CH, white emTrace on navy) in the header of every page, and a matching CH favicon; both are SVG files in assets/img. |
| 0.12.3 | 31 Oct 2026 | Spread map: support layers moved into a panel that slides out over the right side of the map when you click Support layers (close with × or Esc); the button shows how many layers are on. |
| 0.12.2 | 31 Oct 2026 | Spread map: direction arrows in green with a white halo; red dot where the runoff reaches a stream; release point and runoff end points in the legend; each support layer shows its legend and source link when switched on; tracer-test layer described correctly (injection points, not flow paths). Manual: full table of data and map layers with provider, use and website. |
| 0.12.1 | 30 Oct 2026 | Explore map no longer shakes when the pointer rests on a boundary: clickable areas keep a fixed shape when highlighted, the river keeps its full clickable width, and the side panel has a fixed height and scrolls inside. Getting started and Version history removed from the manual page (history moved to this file). |
| 0.12.0 | 30 Oct 2026 | Renamed from Swiss Metal Pathways to CHemTrace, to make room for more contaminants (in progress); contact address added; new icon. |
| 0.11.1 | 30 Oct 2026 | Explore mode: wider river, easier to click; line weights vary more; after a condition switch the changed flows are highlighted with their factor and summarised in a note. |
| 0.11.0 | 28 Oct 2026 | Air and land surface: deposition by particle size, release height, wind and rain; wind-shaped deposition zone on the spread map; most exposed point compared with the LRV; forests and Hg leaf litter; snow, firn and Hg re-emission from snow; Hg⁰ emission from soil and blown dust; sealed surfaces and road-runoff drainage; road traffic and glacier melt as release types; PM10 and PM2.5 from FOEN maps and NABEL background; 18 automated code checks. |
| 0.10.0 | 28 Oct 2026 | Repositioned as an overview for a first plan. Lake trophic state and season; Hg⁰ escape and methylmercury breakdown in sunlit lake water; optional redox ladder for soil and aquifer; exports for PHREEQC, HYDRUS-1D and MODFLOW 6 and a full JSON; 15 automated code checks. |
| 0.9.0 | 27 Oct 2026 | Results rounded to two significant figures; intended-use statement; version on page and reports; assumptions page; automated code checks; softer wording of actions. |
| 0.8 | 27 Oct 2026 | Forms by phase in five scenarios; spread map with time slider, user-set groundwater direction and cantonal and federal support layers; mass budget over time; slope from swisstopo terrain. |
| 0.7 | 27 Oct 2026 | Mass budget with floods; sensitivity chart; validation against published Swiss data; lake trapping and residence time as inputs. |
| 0.6 | 26 Oct 2026 | Self-hosted website; site lookup from national datasets on a 1 km grid; user manual. |
