Screening results

Intended use. An overview of the whole system: where the metal goes, which place to act on first and what to measure, so you can form assumptions and a first plan. Check that plan with site data and the specialised models under Take it further. Also for teaching and comparing scenarios. Not for deciding under the AltlV whether a site must be investigated further or remediated, designing remediation, predicting concentrations or arrival times at a specific well, or individual health-risk assessments. Several defaults are assumptions that experts have not yet reviewed.

      Where and how to trap it

        Take it further: specialised models

        This site gives the overview of the whole system: where the metal goes, which place to act on first and what to measure. Once you have a first plan, check the part that matters with a specialised model. Each file is a starting point filled with your current inputs; values marked ASSUMED must be replaced with site data.

        PHREEQC USGS, free

        Speciation, mineral solubility and sorption on iron oxides, then a 1D column to the water table or the receptor. Checks the Kd and seepage concentration used here.

        You still need: a major-ion analysis, alkalinity and Eh.

        HYDRUS-1D PC-Progress, free version

        Water and metal moving through the unsaturated soil with real weather: arrival time and concentration at the water table.

        You still need: soil texture or hydraulic properties, daily weather and a measured Kd.

        MODFLOW 6 with transport USGS, free, via FloPy

        Groundwater flow and the plume in plan view: which well, spring or river is reached, when, and how large the area above the limit is.

        You still need: the water-table map, boundaries, pumping and the K field.

        All inputs and results JSON

        Every input, result, uncertainty range, mass budget and form split of this run, for records, other tools or rerunning it with SMP.run.

        Open with any text editor or script.

        The files carry the screening values so you can compare. They are tested with PHREEQC 3 (minteq.v4.dat) and MODFLOW 6.7 with FloPy 3.11. The HYDRUS-1D sheet lists the values to enter in its dialogs, because HYDRUS project files depend on the program version.

        From explainer to model

        This page is the knowledge layer: qualitative, rule-based, and checked against literature. The steps below turn it into a quantitative tool.

        1. Knowledge layer (Explore mode)Forms, pathways, risks and trapping options per compartment and scenario.
        2. Screening calculator (Advanced mode)Release and site inputs, Kd-based soil mass balance, leaching, dilution, river partitioning and exposure checks against Swiss values.
        3. Automatic site lookupMap click fills precipitation, soil pH, organic carbon, aquifer type, land use and background values from Swiss national datasets (1 km grid).
        4. Speciation and transport enginesPHREEQC for pH, redox and DOC-dependent speciation; HYDRUS-type vadose transport; a lake oxygen module for Hg methylation.
        5. Uncertainty and validationMonte Carlo ranges; test against NABO, NAQUA, NADUF and case data from Visp and shooting ranges.
